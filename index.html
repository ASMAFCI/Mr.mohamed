About Me

Experience That Makes a Difference.

Mohamed Abdallah Ibrahim

With over 33 years of teaching experience, Mohamed Abdallah Ibrahim is a dedicated and experienced English Language Teacher who believes that effective teaching should be clear, engaging, and enjoyable.

He graduated from Assiut University in 1993 with a Bachelor of Arts and Education and has built a long teaching career in Egypt and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

From 2002 to 2010, he worked in Saudi Arabia as a Senior English Language Teacher, gaining valuable experience in different educational environments.

His teaching style focuses on making lessons simple, engaging, and easy to understand, helping students stay interested and actively participate in the learning process.
